Kalshi Structured Scanner

A read-only terminal over live Kalshi markets across 10 sports. It flags executable pricing mismatches and writes each one up as a plain buy-only plan, ranked best-first. It shows you the trade — it never places one.

GROSS · TOP-OF-BOOK · $1 BASIS · READ-ONLY · NO ORDER ENTRY · NOT RISKLESS · fees est. only

01What it finds

Two kinds of mispricing across related contracts — both always written as buys, never sells:

  • Containment cross — a more specific outcome priced above the broader one that contains it ("win the tournament" can't cost more than "reach the final").
  • Dutch book — a set where exactly one outcome must happen, yet you can buy every outcome for less than the guaranteed payout.

02The whole app in five steps

  1. Open it. You land straight on the scanner — the ranked table of opportunities.
  2. Look at ACTIONABLE first. Those are firm, fillable crosses right now. Everything else is SPECULATIVE (can lose money) or DIAGNOSTIC (coverage only) — leave them until you want them.
  3. Click a row. Its full trade card opens in the Inspector — the legs, the economics, the fees and the settlement rules.
  4. Read the card top-down: the two buys → the guaranteed-payout floor → the gross gap → the fees. The one rule that matters: a gross edge is not a profit — a +1¢ cross can turn net-negative once the taker fee is in, and the card shows gross and net side by side.
  5. Trade it on Kalshi. Hit Market ↗ to open the real contract and place the order yourself; re-SCAN for fresh prices and watch the snapshot age in the stats bar.

04Going faster

Ctrl+K (or /) opens the command palette — jump to any function, participant, lens or layout. J/K move rows, ↵ opens one, 1–6 switch the ranking lens. Settings toggles net-of-fees, auto-refresh, time zone and theme; CSV / ZIP export the current view. Every edge is gross and top-of-book — treat it as a ceiling, not a promise.

Read-only view over a periodically refreshed snapshot of public Kalshi data across 10 sports. The same engine also powers a legacy dashboard at /dashboard. Edges are gross and top-of-book; fees are an estimate; nothing here is order entry or trading advice.
